FUNCTION BEFORE APPEARANCE

A smaller path to individualized review

Start with the asserted risk. Understand the engineering system. Check primary sources. Ask a governed question. Apply a transparent method with effective, less-restrictive alternatives.

Long-life mobility design

Design mobility across decades

If substantially longer human lifespans become common, mobility systems will need to consider cumulative burden across decades rather than assuming conventional walking is the only legitimate form of ambulation.

Rolling and alternative movement strategies deserve individualized evaluation as possible adaptations. Unfamiliar form does not establish actual risk, and a familiar substitute does not establish equivalent function.

Cumulative burden

Review repetition, impact, posture, transportation, recovery, and practical task demands across time—not only a brief encounter.

Function across life

Ask which strategy preserves meaningful mobility as capacity, environment, technology, and support needs change.

Alternative movement

Compare walking, rolling, assistive devices, transportation, and environmental redesign through individualized evidence.

This is a design and policy principle, not evidence that any particular mobility aid extends lifespan or prevents degeneration.
