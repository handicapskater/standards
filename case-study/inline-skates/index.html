MOTIVATING CASE · BOUNDED EXAMPLE

Inline skates as a non-standard mobility aid

This case motivates a general protocol. It does not make inline skates the rule, establish a universal device classification, or replace setting-specific evaluation.

What the case illustrates

Function can emerge before a familiar device pathway exists.

A functional adaptation developed outside a conventional prescription path. Historical documentation preceded modern instrumentation, and optional modern evidence later supported review of present-day function without becoming a prerequisite.

CASE-DERIVED LESSON

Function

A person may develop an assistive mobility strategy through lived function rather than a conventional device pathway.

CASE-DERIVED LESSON

Environment

Different institutions can reach different scoped decisions because the relevant setting and hazard profile differ.

CASE-DERIVED LESSON

Documentation

Prior documentation and optional evidence can inform review without converting one person’s record into a universal rule.

Standards lesson

Evaluate function, environment, risk, conditions, and the documented decision.

The case supports a general inquiry: what function does the aid provide, what is the actual environment, what specific risk is supported, what conditions may address it, and what decision should be documented?
