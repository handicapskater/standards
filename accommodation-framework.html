Review framework

Individualized accommodation review

A practical checklist for agencies, transit providers, businesses, courts, public entities, and accessibility reviewers.

An accommodation review should distinguish discomfort with non-standard mobility aids from evidence of actual direct threat.

Accommodation Review Sequence

Use this sequence: identify the disability related mobility use, identify the device function, review physical accommodation, evaluate actual direct threat, consider narrower restrictions, compare effective alternatives, and document the individualized conclusion.

For transportation-specific review, use the Transportation Accommodation page to evaluate vehicle mode, seat position, shared routing, route duration, posture, reliability, feasible rail connections, and lower burden alternatives.
