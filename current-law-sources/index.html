Current law / guidance

Keep agencies and source scope distinct.

DOJ

DOJ mobility-device guidance describes mobility-device categories and, in its powered-device context, actual-risk safety requirements and setting factors. It does not classify inline skates as OPDMDs or establish this full protocol.

Protocol boundary

What this site does not claim.

The Portable Mobility Record does not exist as a general federal certification program. The protocol is not a permit, a diagnosis framework, a legal entitlement, a mandatory mitigation rule, or a substitute for individualized review.
