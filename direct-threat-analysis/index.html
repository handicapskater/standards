ACTUAL RISK AND INDIVIDUALIZED REVIEW

Direct-Threat Analysis

Assess significant risk through individualized, setting-specific evidence and consider whether reasonable measures reduce it.

Review the analysis questionsIndividual access history

Document the asserted risk

Identify the nature, duration, severity, likelihood, and imminence of the claimed harm; the evidence supporting those judgments; the actual environment; and the individual’s demonstrated control and accommodation needs.

Evidence to seek

  • Current, objective, setting-specific facts
  • Individual function and demonstrated operation
  • Environmental constraints and credible incident context
  • Uncertainty, missing information, and source authority

Alternatives to test

  • Operational conditions or route adjustments
  • Timing, positioning, storage, or assistance
  • Effective alternative aids or transportation
  • Less-restrictive measures that address the actual risk

Environment-Specific Review

Unfamiliarity, appearance, or ordinary recreational use cannot substitute for individualized facts; the documented setting and governing authority determine the decision. This generalized framework does not decide any case or establish binding law.
