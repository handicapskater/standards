Source-linked standards references

Public source anchors for the HandicapSkater.org standards framework, including DOT/FTA mobility-device history, DOJ OPDMD regulations and guidance, and ADA Title II / Title III review principles.

Core federal timeline sources

These sources support the 2005 / 2007 / 2010 timeline used across the .org site.

2005 DOT guidance context

DOT later described its September 2005 guidance as addressing non-standard mobility devices, including Segways, and requiring acceptance where the device can be physically accommodated and does not create a direct threat to safety.

Federal Register: DOT discussion of 2005 non-standard mobility-device guidance

2007 DOT/FTA BART determination

FTA’s October 15, 2007 BART determination is the public federal source for the roller skates as mobility aid analysis used in this standards timeline. This site uses “skates” or “inline skates” for the current standards framing while preserving historical source terminology where needed.

FTA: Bay Area Rapid Transit District, San Francisco, CA, 10-15-07

2010 DOJ ADA rules

DOJ published revised ADA Title II and Title III regulations in the Federal Register on September 15, 2010, adopting the 2010 ADA Standards and updating the mobility-device framework.

ADA.gov: 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design

DOJ OPDMD regulations and public guidance

OPDMD is power-driven-device language. It is important to keep this framework distinct from the 2007 FTA. This site uses “skates” or “inline skates” for current standards framing while preserving historical source terminology where needed.

DOJ mobility-devices guidance

DOJ explains that ADA mobility devices fall into two broad categories: wheelchairs and manually powered devices, and other powered options such as golf carts, Segways, and similar devices.

ADA.gov: Mobility Devices

DOJ OPDMD technical assistance

DOJ guidance explains the rules for wheelchairs, mobility aids, and other power-driven mobility devices for state and local governments, businesses, and nonprofits serving the public.

ADA.gov: Wheelchairs, Mobility Aids, and Other Power-Driven Mobility Devices

ADA Title II and Title III regulatory anchors

These are the primary DOJ regulatory pages for public entities and public accommodations.

Title II regulations

Title II covers state and local government services. The OPDMD provisions include assessment factors and credible-assurance rules for public entities.

ADA.gov: Title II 2010 Regulations

Title III regulations

Title III covers public accommodations. The OPDMD provisions include definitions, assessment factors, and limits on when safety-based restrictions may be used.

ADA.gov: Title III Regulations

Transportation and FTA accessibility guidance

These sources provide broader transit-access context for mobility devices, facilities, vehicles, lifts, ramps, and ADA transit operations.

FTA ADA Circular 4710.1

FTA’s ADA circular is a major transit guidance source for ADA obligations in federally assisted public transportation.

FTA Circular 4710.1: Americans with Disabilities Act Guidance

National RTAP transit ADA toolkit

National RTAP summarizes practical transit requirements for riders who use wheelchairs and other mobility devices, drawing on DOT ADA regulations and FTA guidance.

National RTAP: Accommodating Riders Using Mobility Devices

How to read these references

The 2005 DOT guidance context, 2007 FTA roller-skates determination, and 2010 DOJ OPDMD rules are related but not identical. This site uses them to explain individualized, environment-specific accommodation review rather than categorical approval or categorical exclusion.

  • 2005 DOT: non-standard mobility-device concept in transportation guidance, especially Segway-type devices.
  • 2007 DOT/FTA: roller skates analyzed as a mobility aid in the BART complaint record.
  • 2010 DOJ: OPDMD regulations for other power-driven mobility devices under ADA Title II and Title III.
