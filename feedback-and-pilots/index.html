Who can use this

Designed for practical institutional review.

Institutions

Transit agencies, courts, airports, state/local government, public accommodations, universities, and employers where relevant.

Reviewers

Disability-rights organizations, attorneys, rehabilitation professionals, researchers, and standards bodies.

Pilots

Small, documented trials that test usability, privacy, scope, conditions, and review consistency without claiming adoption.

Pilot questions

What should a pilot test?

Whether the eight stages are understandable, whether evidence demands are proportionate, whether mitigation options are workable, whether records are privacy-minimizing, and whether different reviewers can explain a scoped decision consistently.
