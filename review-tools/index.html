PROPOSED REVIEW TOOLS

Make the review practical.

Printable, institution-neutral templates for documenting a function-first, setting-specific decision. These tools are proposed standard materials, not mandatory legal forms.

Tool set

Use the tools that fit the decision.

No single form replaces individualized judgment. A demonstration is not a universal obstacle-course requirement.

Functional Intake

Requested function, limited task, use without the aid, alternatives, and minimum necessary context.

  • Task limited
  • Requested function
  • Functional equivalence of alternatives

Device / Adaptation Profile

Movement mechanism, control, stopping, steering, transfers, stability, dimensions, speed, storage, and operating characteristics.

Proficiency Checklist

Experience, operating history, control, stopping, maneuvering, transfers, and setting-specific conditions where relevant.

No universal proficiency test.

Environment Checklist

Surface, grade, width, crowding, boarding, transfers, security, storage, emergency egress, and vehicle interaction.

Actual-Risk Worksheet

Hazard, mechanism, likelihood, severity, user control, safeguards, mitigation, uncertainty, residual risk, and scoped decision.

Open the framework

Mitigation Worksheet

Potential conditions, operational feasibility, evidence support, residual risk, and reasons for accepting or rejecting each condition.

Decision Record

Request, evidence, setting, risks, mitigation, decision, conditions, scope, reviewer, date, review route, and reassessment trigger.

Review Stage 8

Portable Mobility Record

A voluntary, privacy-minimizing summary of function, device, scoped decisions, conditions, and provenance references.

Review the PMR model

Reassessment Form

Identify changed material facts, preserve unchanged findings, record revised conditions, and avoid automatic restart from zero.

Printable and accessible

Information should not exist only in a diagram.

Each tool is expressed in plain language and can be printed. Future fillable versions should preserve keyboard access, clear labels, visible focus, and a text alternative for every diagram or matrix.
