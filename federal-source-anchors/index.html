Purpose

Why anchor review in primary sources?

Primary documents distinguish binding text, agency guidance, individual determinations, historical terminology, and later updates.

AuthorityWho issued the source, under what jurisdiction, and with what legal status?
ContextWhich device, person, setting, date, and asserted risk did the source address?
LimitWhat does the source not decide, and what later material may change its use?

A federal source anchor is a primary government document preserved with enough metadata and context for a reviewer to understand what it says and how narrowly it applies.

Anchor
Issuer, title, date, URL, jurisdiction, status, terminology, and relevant setting.
Qualification
A source is not converted into categorical approval, categorical exclusion, or a universal rule.

Chronology

How did the terminology and settings change?

Related materials use different terms and contexts. They should not be collapsed into one universal rule.

DOT guidance discussed Segways used by people with disabilities and the non-standard mobility-device concept in transportation settings.

2007 DOT/FTA

Roller skates analyzed as a mobility aid

The FTA BART complaint determination addressed skates in a particular transit environment. It should not be presented as categorical approval for every setting.

Read the FTA source

OPDMD regulations

DOJ adopted other power-driven mobility-device provisions. OPDMD is powered-device language; it is distinct from the earlier skates analysis.

Source review

How should authority be evaluated?

Record the issuing body, publication date, status, jurisdiction, quoted language, context, later updates, and limitations. Do not convert agency guidance or an individual determination into a categorical rule without authority for that step.

Keep legal and scientific records separate

Scientific observations are inspected in the Evidence Observatory; federal sources are interpreted in their own legal and historical context.

Separate claim classes

What kind of proposition is being reviewed?

Scientific findingMeasured or derived N-of-1 evidence belongs to the Evidence Observatory with its source, sample, and metric scope.
Documented historyAn approved source may document an access denial or agency decision within its actual record and scope.
Legal argument / user positionAn allegation, accommodation claim, or unresolved accessibility dispute remains identified as a position unless an authoritative adjudication establishes it.
