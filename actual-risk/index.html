PRINCIPLE 02 · EVIDENCE BEFORE ASSUMPTION

Actual Risk

Name the specific harm, document the setting, evaluate likelihood and severity, and distinguish observed facts from unfamiliar appearance.

Apply the principleDirect-threat framework

Make the asserted risk testable

A review should identify the person, task, device, route, environment, duration, control strategy, alleged harm, and evidence supporting the alleged likelihood. A label such as unusual, recreational, or unsafe is not a risk analysis.

Observed

Record current, objective, setting-specific facts and the source of each fact.

Uncertain

Keep missing information, conflicting accounts, and limits visible rather than filling the gap with inference.

Reducible

Test operational conditions, assistance, route changes, positioning, timing, and less-restrictive alternatives.

Risk is not a scientific score

Wearable and movement evidence may inform a setting-specific review, but it does not decide legal entitlement, directly measure pain, or establish universal safety. Scientific questions and evidence remain in the Evidence Observatory.
