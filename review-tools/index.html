STAGE 6 · PROPOSED STANDARD

Actual-Risk Worksheet

Record specific, setting-specific hazards and visible uncertainty. Qualitative values are not a mathematical risk score.

STAGE 7 · PROPOSED STANDARD

Mitigation Worksheet

NARROWEST EFFECTIVE CONDITION is a PROPOSED STANDARD, not universal law. Optional prompts: pedestrian-speed operation, restricted zone, narrowly defined dismount point, escort, alternate boarding, storage procedure, security procedure, designated path, or justified time/place condition.
