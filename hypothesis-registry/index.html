Hypothesis Registry

Begin with a versioned question, comparison, governed metrics, interpretation order, limitations, and provenance.

Purpose

Why register a hypothesis before reading results?

It keeps the scientific question, comparison, metrics, exclusions, limitations, and allowed interpretation from drifting after results are visible.

PredefineState the question and comparison before selecting a conclusion.
GovernSeparate primary, supporting, derived, exploratory, and context measures.
ConstrainAttach scope, prohibited claims, review status, and version history.

Definition

What is a hypothesis registry?

A hypothesis registry is a versioned protocol record that connects a scientific question to comparison cohorts, eligibility rules, metric roles, required figures, interpretation rules, limitations, provenance, and review state.

Required order
Observed Result → Integrated Interpretation → Scope → Accommodation Relevance.
Authority
